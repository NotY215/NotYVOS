Role in NOTYVOS
---------------

VirtualBox is used as a testing tool by the NOTYVOS developers. It is not
part of NOTYVOS source code, is not shipped with NOTYVOS, and is not
linked against any NOTYVOS component. It runs on the host machine, not
inside NOTYVOS.

NOTYVOS source is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. AGPL-3.0 is compatible
with GPL-3.0 for the manner in which NOTYVOS uses VirtualBox: separate
host software, not linked or redistributed together.

If you distribute a NOTYVOS disk image or ISO to another developer, you
do not distribute VirtualBox. The developer installs VirtualBox
themselves from the official upstream source.
