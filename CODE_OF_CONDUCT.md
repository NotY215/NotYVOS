# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NotYVOS is an independent, experimental open-source project. We want participation to be welcoming, respectful, constructive, and accessible to people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas, code, and technical decisions without attacking people.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Focus discussions on improving the project and helping others learn.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexual harassment, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Deliberately posting malicious content, spam, or confidential information is also prohibited.

## Project scope and enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for handling reports and deciding appropriate responses. Depending on severity, actions may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer through the contact options listed on the GitHub profile at https://github.com/NotY215. Please include relevant links or context and avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily. Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing or participating in the NotYVOS community.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by common open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant. It is a project-specific policy and does not claim to reproduce or adopt another project's policy verbatim.
